// about_me

Hi, I'm Martyn — a developer based in Scotland who has spent a good chunk of the last couple of decades building things for the web. I started out in PHP (Laravel/Slim), but have experience working in other languages/frameworks such as JavaScript (Vue/Nuxt/Next.js), Java (Spring Boot) and Python.

I like tidy code, sensible infrastructure (containers, please), and lately I've been spending my spare time on security tooling and AI experiments.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm probably tinkering with a side project I'll definitely finish one day.

04.gaming

// insert coin to continue

I'm a gamer from the 90s — I grew up on chunky pixels, chiptune soundtracks and games you could pick up in seconds and still be playing at 2am. That era still shapes what I love about games: tight controls, simple ideas and a lot of charm.

These days I don't just play them, I make them. I'm the developer of Tiny Football, a retro-style football game built with the Godot engine — fast, top-down, and very much inspired by the games I played as a kid.

Retro-style games are my favourite thing to build: pixel art, small worlds and gameplay that's easy to pick up and hard to put down.
